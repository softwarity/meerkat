Authentication

Every door into the data plane, the accounts held here included. An authority proves who someone is; it never decides what they may do: a first sign-in creates an account that reaches nothing until an administrator places it in an organization and grants roles. Switch them all off and nobody signs in to the data plane, which is what a gateway serving public routes only may want.
